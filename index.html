Introduction

About Me

I am a student at West Shore Jr./Sr. High School in Melbourne, Florida, with interests spanning medicine, biomedical engineering, community service, music, and collaborative problem-solving.

My long-term goal is to become a cardiothoracic surgeon-scientist. I am especially interested in developing tissue-engineered solutions that can improve cardiovascular treatment while providing compassionate, life-saving care to patients.

Beyond the laboratory, I enjoy creating programs that connect students with seniors, educating others about brain health, performing music in healthcare settings, and helping young people develop leadership and problem-solving skills.

Recent Project

One recent project examined pH-responsive particles capable of delivering pro-angiogenic substances, including carbon monoxide, under acidic conditions associated with ischemia developing or returning within vascular grafts.

I have conducted laboratory experiments, synthesized particles, analyzed data, and contributed to project development intended to improve vascular-graft materials for medical applications.

I am also developing an individual contribution to a bioprinting project.
