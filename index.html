I collaborate with Dr. Bashur at Florida Institute of Technology on research focused on promoting endothelialization and improving the long-term patency of vascular tissue-engineered grafts.

Laboratory Experience

Project 1 · pH-Responsive Particle Delivery

In 2025, I developed pH-responsive particles designed to deliver pro-angiogenic substances such as carbon monoxide under acidic conditions associated with ischemia in vascular grafts. I synthesized particles, operated laboratory equipment, and analyzed experimental data.

Project 2 · Bioprinted Gradient Collagen Sleeve

In 2026, I investigated the spatio-temporal availability of transforming growth factor-β3 within a bioprinted gradient collagen sleeve to promote chondrogenic differentiation in the context of ACL reconstruction. I cultured cells, quantified 100+ images, created graphs, curated publication figures, and performed statistical tests.

Research milestone: This project is pending publication, and I am scheduled to give an oral presentation at the TERMIS-AM Annual Conference in November 2026.
